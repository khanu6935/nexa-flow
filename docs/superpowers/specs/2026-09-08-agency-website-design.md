# NexaFlow Agency Website Design

Build a premium international software-agency website positioned around end-to-end technology delivery: design, build, automate, test, deploy and support. The homepage follows the approved 14-section sequence exactly and avoids fake clients, statistics, awards or testimonials.

Architecture: Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS. Content is data-driven through service, project, industry and technology modules. Shared layout and section components are reusable, while forms and the chatbot use a common lead submission abstraction that can later target a real backend.

Primary routes: `/`, `/services`, `/solutions`, `/portfolio`, `/portfolio/[slug]`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`.
